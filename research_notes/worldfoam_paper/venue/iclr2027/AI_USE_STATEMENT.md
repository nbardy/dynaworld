# Statement on the use of generative AI

Generative-AI tools, including large language models used through ChatGPT and
OpenAI Codex, played a significant supporting role in this project. They were
used for mathematical formulation and red-team analysis, research planning,
code drafting and refactoring, test and artifact-verifier scaffolding,
experiment orchestration, figure generation, literature-query assistance, and
manuscript editing. Their proposals were treated as hypotheses or drafts and
were required to survive explicit counterexamples, executable checks, and
source-bound evidence gates before entering the paper.

The author reviewed the resulting code, derivations, experiment contracts,
citations, figures, and manuscript text and is responsible for every claim in
the submission. Numerical results reported in the paper must come from
executed artifacts that pass the repository's independent verifiers;
model-generated numbers, source-only implementations, smoke tests, and failed
or incomplete artifacts are excluded. Generative-AI systems are not authors
and bear no responsibility for the work.
