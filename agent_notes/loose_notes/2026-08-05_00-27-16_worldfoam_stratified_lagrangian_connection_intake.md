## Decisive corrections

- The external note uses `partial_z U=A_z U`, opposite to WorldFoam's
  near-to-far scan. The executable path appends far segments on the right:

  ```text
  U(b,a)=U(s,a) U(b,s),
  partial_b U=U A_z(b).
  ```

- The repo-native curvature is

  ```text
  F^R_tz=partial_t A_z-partial_z A_t+[A_t,A_z].
  ```

  Its exact fixed-endpoint identity is

  ```text
  partial_t U-U A_t(b)+A_t(a) U
    = integral U(s,a) F^R_tz(s) U(b,s) ds.
  ```

- With moving endpoints, the missing terms are

  ```text
  U[A_t+b_dot A_z]_b-[A_t+a_dot A_z]_a U.
  ```

- The constrained choice `A_t=-wA_z` still yields the scientist's useful
  continuity residual:

  ```text
  F^R_tz=partial_t A_z+partial_z(wA_z).
  ```

- For P0 interfaces, the general singular coefficient is

  ```text
  [wA_z]-r_dot[A_z],
  ```

  not `(w-r_dot)[A_z]` unless `w` has a continuous boundary trace.

- Coordinate-depth extinction must include the physical ray Jacobian
  `||partial_z Gamma||`.

- Flatness is equivalent to reuse of every flow-transported subinterval under
  a specified non-crossing flow. It is not necessary for equality of one total
  transfer; nonzero curvature can cancel in depth.

- A scalar depth flow on a fixed pixel cannot represent generic transverse 3D
  motion. General coherent motion needs a full sensor-depth horizontal field
  or tracked pixel path.

- An unrestricted per-ray flow can hide the complete opacity movie. Flow must
  be independently generated by a compact scene/camera motion basis and its
  state, fit, endpoint, and gradient costs must be charged.

- The element-valued interval assignment in the scientist note is not a
  factorization algebra/cosheaf. The exact safe object is ordered multiplicative
  interval transport over adjacent partitions.

- The order-`q` seam formula needs one-sided `C^q` germs, separate signed width
  coefficients, and matching exterior jets.

- Cumulative seam-defect sets grow with jet order:

  ```text
  Sigma_<=0 subseteq Sigma_<=1 subseteq ... subseteq Delta_owner.
  ```

  The source note stated the relation in the wrong direction.

- Separated interior real simple roots have canonical sorted labels and
  trivial real-order monodromy. Complex braid and 2-stack machinery is not a
  current runtime need.

measured request time by at least 20% on representative real charts.
