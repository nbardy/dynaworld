Main lesson:

EoH adds the operator taxonomy the local runner was missing. Store a short
candidate thought and operator name with every candidate. Useful operator labels
are `explore_different`, `extract_common_backbone`, `improve_parent`,
`retune_constants`, and `simplify_patch`.

Specific DynaWorld implications:

- Thoughts are prompt material, not evidence. The evaluator remains the source
  of truth.
- Use expert seeds: current hand-written baselines, benchmark-positive
  prototypes, and good near misses should enter the initial population.
- Add simplification as a first-class operator because evolutionary patch search
  will otherwise accumulate brittle code.
- EoH-style operators fit `gaussian_512_promotion_guard`,
  `mixed_same_view_novel_scheduler`, and `code_org_helpers` better than the
  broad STAR UVT kernel path.
