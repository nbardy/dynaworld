# Gate4 coeff16 cap224 specialization reject

## Context

The previous ownerupdate/sortnet/i16 forks showed that extra candidate-side
streams and owner-transition bookkeeping do not beat the sample-parallel
coeff16 keeper. This fork tested a narrower idea: keep the exact same tape and
sample-parallel math, but specialize the hot Metal kernel for the measured
Gate4 site24 row cap.

The new mode is:

```text
gate4-affine-candidate-coeff16-cap224-fused-mse
```

It uses the same `candidate_row_offsets_i32` and `candidate_depth_coeff_f16`
tape as the keeper, but shrinks the per-thread local depth/segment arrays from
`256/257` to `224/225`. The recent site24 artifacts had max row sizes
`220-224`, so this should preserve the measured gate while potentially
reducing local memory/register pressure.

## Implementation

Added:

- Metal kernel:
  `wf2_fused_slab_affine_coeff16_cap224_mse_vjp_direct_atomic_rgb_only_tensor`
- C++/Metal launcher and op:
  `fused_slab_affine_coeff16_cap224_mse_vjp_direct_atomic_rgb_only`
- Python wrapper with CSR row-length validation capped at `224`
- Train/eval tape mode:
  `gate4-affine-candidate-coeff16-cap224-fused-mse`
- Verifier flag:
  `gate4_affine_candidate_csr_cap224_fused_mse`
- MPS parity coverage against the sample-parallel keeper.

Artifacts:

- cap224:
  `research_experiments/world_foam_lane2/results/2026-05-19_gate4_affine_candidate_coeff16_cap224_scale_2_4_8_16_render16_site24_warm3.json`
- same-window sample keeper:

| 2 | 5.045 | 5.100 | 1.011x | 4.534 | 4.557 | 1.005x | 1.000x |
| 4 | 4.417 | 2.807 | 0.635x | 3.926 | 2.384 | 0.607x | 1.000x |

The cap224 artifact is individually sublinear:

- total step scale: `0.9344`
- backward scale: `0.9178`

But the sample keeper is also sublinear and has better scale:

- total step scale: `0.8832`
- backward scale: `0.8592`

## Decision

Do not promote cap224. It is correctness-green and storage-neutral, but the
paired timing is mixed rather than a real win. The 4f row is much faster, while
8f is much slower and 16f is also slower. Under a contended environment this is
not enough evidence to replace the keeper.

The useful lesson is that simply shrinking the per-thread arrays from
`256/257` to `224/225` does not reliably improve the Gate4 shape. The next
fork should attack the actual per-sample work rather than local-array footprint
alone.
