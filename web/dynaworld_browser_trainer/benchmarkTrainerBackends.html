DynaWorld Browser Trainer Backend Benchmark

Runs both registered SPA training backends sequentially against one loaded Coffee Martini train17/holdout1 bundle and the same requested splat count.

The backends are not objective-equivalent: tiled3d supervises a complete image with L1 + SSIM, while sampled3d supervises selected rays with RGB MSE and support guards. Steps/s and supervised pixels/s describe throughput, not equal optimization progress or quality.

Each backend gets a fresh trainer and WebGPU device. Dataset loading, shader compilation, buffer initialization, warmup, rendering, validation, and metric readback are excluded. The measured interval includes JavaScript command encoding and submission, and ends after queue.onSubmittedWorkDone() confirms that every measured training step completed on the GPU. Tiled growth headroom is disabled here so both lanes optimize the requested number of splats. Raster scales above 1 use nearest-neighbor copies of the loaded 96x72 targets solely to measure kernel scaling; they are not higher-detail training data. The SSIM control changes the tiled backend's local box window for throughput ablation only. Packed FP16 affects only forward compositing checkpoints; projection, loss, gradients, and optimizer state remain FP32.
